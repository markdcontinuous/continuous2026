### How we got to the OpCon V2 lesson format
The problem: V1 shipped 94 SCORM slide decks built with the Webslide kit; V2 is new content and Mark wanted to stop making SCORM. Options considered: (1) V1 slide decks without SCORM, with the V2 narration scripts as audio or a text panel; (2) a scrolling page in V1's look; (3) the BBE 101 eLearning page (header, topic tabs, Knowledge Check tab). Rejected: slides (Mark: "this won't be slides"); narration (Mark: none, because there is no SCORM); BBE's video "Watch" step (no videos). Mark chose (3): "Visual is like BBE". The reading text comes from the V2 scripts, lightly edited, because the on-slide text alone is only bullets.
