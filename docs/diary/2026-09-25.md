## How we got to 15 courses rather than 15 lessons

Skilljar only categorises at course level: a lesson lives inside a course and
never appears on the catalog. So "1 video = 1 lesson" only produces tiles to
categorise if each video is its own course with a single lesson inside it. That
is what was built. The five source courses that had lessons map onto four
existing section keys, so the catalog script needs no change: start gets six
(Getting started plus Account and licensing, which has no section of its own),
jobs, triggers and connections get three each.
